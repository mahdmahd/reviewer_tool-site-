Six stations. Two reads kept apart.

Get to a final peer review report with six easy steps, of which two are optional. We guide you through so you can focus on the science.

01

Invite

Ask yourself three easy questions to decide whether you should accept this peer review: expertise, conflicts of interest and time.

02

First read

Read the manuscript entirely and jot down anything that comes to mind. Don't worry about nice wording, minor/major classification, or whether you will include it in the review letter eventually.

03

Second read

Read the manuscript again after having detached from it (e.g. read it the next day). This phase is about filtering your initial notes, adding new ones, rewriting the ones you keep, and labelling them.

04

AI Checklist

Are you reviewing a paper involving AI in healthcare? This optional step contains a list of questions, based on EQUATOR checklists, that aims to filter out major flaws in AI methodology and reporting.

Optional
05

Report

View your entire report as it will appear as PDF. Make final adaptations, or choose for AI polishing in step 6.

06

AI polishing

If you would like to have your text refined with the help of AI, you can provide your own model key or have us generate a prompt for you to copy into your favourite chat app.

Optional
AI Glossary

A term you don't know never stops the reading.

Every step carries an AI Glossary button — a searchable list of AI and research-ethics terms, from algorithmic bias to TRIPOD+AI, one click away whenever a word in the paper (or in your own head) needs defining.

Generative AI: where we let it in.

AI can sharpen how you say what you decided. It must never decide for you. Here is how this tool guides a reviewer through that maze — and exactly where the barrier is drawn.

Bring your own key

Paste the API base URL and key for a model you already use — OpenAI-compatible or Gemini-compatible endpoints. The tool fetches that endpoint's own model list and you pick one.

Request goes machine → endpoint Key held in this browser, clearable in one click Output previewed beside your draft before anything is applied

Copy a ready prompt out

Generate a complete prompt with your finished report already embedded, fenced and ruled. Paste it into whichever assistant you prefer, then paste the result back to compare against your draft.

No key, no endpoint, no account Built locally and offline Same side-by-side comparison when you paste the result back

The barrier is drawn where the manuscript ends.

Peer review is conducted in confidence. Journals generally forbid sharing an unpublished manuscript with third parties, and that includes model providers. So this tool does not ask you to trust a privacy policy.

The polish step sees the draft report you would submit — nothing else. That report is built from your readings, so your own wording travels inside it. What does not travel is the manuscript: the tool has no field for manuscript text, so it cannot send what it has never held. The only manuscript-derived string in a request is the identifier you typed into the report header yourself.

If the journal's rules leave no room for even that, take the copy-out route instead. It builds the prompt locally and sends nothing at all.

Verify it yourself: the request is made from your own browser, and the developer tools will show you exactly what was in it.
